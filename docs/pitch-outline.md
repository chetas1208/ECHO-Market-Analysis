# ECHO pitch outline

1. **Problem** — Headlines are priced faster than their economic consequences
   propagate.
2. **Insight** — The opportunity is the residual between expected propagation
   and absorbed price response.
3. **ECHO** — Event sensing, economic relationships, probabilistic forecasts,
   immutable verification.
4. **Live event → graph** — Hormuz → oil/shipping → energy and airline exposure.
5. **Forecast** — Abnormal-return distribution with q10/q50/q90 and cutoff.
6. **Prediction ledger** — A forecast vintage stays frozen while reality appends.
7. **Strategy / results** — Deterministic risk gate and replay metrics, with
   explicit insufficient-data limits.
8. **Architecture** — World/market feeds → events → ShockGraph → forecast → risk
   gate → abstain or paper trade.
9. **Why it wins** — Causal provenance plus uncertainty plus auditability, not
   sentiment theater.
10. **Close** — ECHO acts only where economically meaningful propagation remains
    incomplete.
